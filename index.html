Mechanism design

Slot reservations

Uncapped competition meant many providers downloading the same content for a single payout, and a lucrative request could DDoS the client that posted it. Capping reservations fixed both. Shipped, with an adversarial analysis covering six named attacker classes and a written record of every mechanism rejected along the way. Read the design →

Protocol design

On-chain marketplace

A storage market where strangers pay strangers to keep data alive: threat modelling and mitigation, incentive and collateral design, then planning, implementation, testing and exploit preparedness. Includes the published protocol specification and a public research repository. Also: a per-state recoverability analysis giving every state in the sales machine a defined outcome under crash, power loss and asynchronous cancellation.

Gas economics

10× cheaper state restoration

Two competing on-chain designs, built and benchmarked rather than argued about. Worst-case slot-freeing fell from 3,115,596 to 302,584 gas. The report priced the difference in euros at live rates and ended the debate. Read the analysis →
